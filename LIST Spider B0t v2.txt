🧠 the brain + camera
ESP32-CAM (AI-Thinker) — core board; Wi-Fi + camera + microSD slot built in
OV2640 NoIR camera variant — 2MP infrared-sensitive camera; works for normal video and can see near-infrared with IR illumination
IR LEDs (850nm or 940nm) — invisible illumination for seeing in darkness
MicroSD card, 8–32GB — local storage for photos/videos on the spider
PCA9685 servo driver — controls all servos over I2C so the camera doesn't run out of GPIO pins


🕷️ legs / movement — UPDATED
12× micro servos, SG90 or MG90S — 3 per leg × 4 legs
1× hip rotation
1× upper-leg movement
1× lower-leg/knee movement
1× PCA9685 16-channel servo driver — enough channels for all 12 servos, with 4 channels left over
3D-printed chassis — palm-sized 4-legged spider design
Servo horns + linkages — connects the servos to the three-segment legs
12× servo mounting hardware — brackets/screws as required by the chassis

⚠️ BIG power warning: 12 servos can draw a lot of current, especially when several move simultaneously. Don't power them from the ESP32-CAM or its 5V pin.


🔋 power
LiPo battery — main power source
High-current 5–6V buck converter for servos — dedicated servo power rail
Separate 5V regulator/buck converter for ESP32-CAM + electronics
TP4056 charging module — USB charging
Common GND between the ESP32 and servo power system — required so the PCA9685 control signals have a shared reference

So the architecture becomes:

Battery → Servo buck → PCA9685 → 12 servos

and separately:

Battery → Electronics buck → ESP32-CAM + OV2640 NoIR + IR LEDs

with GND connected between the two sides.


🌙 infrared / night vision
OV2640 NoIR variant — detects near-infrared because it doesn't have the normal IR-cut filter
850nm or 940nm IR LEDs — illuminate the environment in darkness
IR LED driver / MOSFET — lets the ESP32 turn the IR LEDs on/off
Optional IR LED ring/board — makes mounting the illumination around the camera easier

This is night vision, not thermal imaging. The camera sees reflected near-infrared light rather than detecting heat.
